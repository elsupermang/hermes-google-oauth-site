Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

This policy describes the personal Hermes AI Digests automation and the static informational website at this domain. The website itself does not collect personal information through forms, accounts, cookies, advertising, or analytics.

Information the automation may access

When the account owner authorizes Hermes with Google OAuth, the local automation may request access to Google services and scopes needed for enabled integrations. Depending on the integrations enabled, these can include Gmail, Calendar, Drive, Contacts, Google Docs, and Google Sheets.

For the AI and e-bike digest jobs, the relevant operation is sending the generated digest through Gmail to the recipient configured by the account owner. The digest jobs do not use Google data for advertising, profiling, or sale.

How Google data is used

  • To authenticate the account owner’s authorized Hermes installation.
  • To send requested digest emails through Gmail.
  • To perform another Google Workspace action only when the corresponding Hermes integration is explicitly enabled by the account owner.

Hermes does not sell Google user data, use it for targeted advertising, or transfer it to data brokers. Google API use is limited to the functionality described here and to the permissions granted on the Google consent screen.

Where data is stored

This website does not store Google OAuth tokens or Google account data. OAuth credentials and automation state are stored locally by the Hermes installation controlled by the account owner. Public news articles and digest state may also be stored locally so that already-delivered items can be deduplicated.

Google processes email and other Workspace data under Google’s own terms and privacy policies when the Google APIs are used.

Sharing

The automation shares data only with the Google services required to complete an action requested by the account owner. Digest summaries may also be delivered to other destinations configured by the account owner, such as Telegram. The static website does not transmit Google account data to those destinations.

Retention and deletion

Local OAuth credentials and automation state remain until the account owner deletes them or revokes access. The account owner can revoke Google access at Google Account third-party access and can delete the local Hermes token and state files from the Hermes installation.

Security

The public website and repository do not contain OAuth client secrets, refresh tokens, access tokens, or other credentials. Users should never publish those values in the repository or in an issue.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated when the automation’s functionality changes. Privacy questions or correction requests can be submitted through the project repository’s issue tracker.
