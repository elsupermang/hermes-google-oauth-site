Hermes AI Digests

Curated news digests for enterprise AI and practical commuter e-bike technology.

What this app does

Hermes collects selected public news sources, prepares concise digests, and delivers them to the destinations configured by the account owner. Email delivery uses Google Gmail authorization granted by the user.

Privacy

This is a personal automation site. It does not provide user accounts, advertising, analytics, tracking pixels, or a web form for collecting information.

Read the Privacy Policy for details about Google API access, local storage, retention, and revocation.
